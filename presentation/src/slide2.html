THE PROBLEM
Why small businesses fall behind

Every business is online now.
Not every business has a marketing team.

01

A team they can’t afford

Good marketing takes a researcher, a creative director, a video editor and an analyst. Most small businesses can’t hire even one.

02

A crowded online market

Almost every business sells online today, so a small shop shares the same feed with brands that have full teams and big budgets.

03

Posting and hoping

Without research or analytics, owners guess what to post and never learn why one video worked and another didn’t.

04

The owner does it all

The same person runs the store, serves customers and makes the content. Marketing gets whatever time is left.
